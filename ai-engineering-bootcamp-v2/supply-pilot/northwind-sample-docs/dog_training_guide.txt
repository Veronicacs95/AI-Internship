==============================================================================
1. BASIC DOG TRAINING
==============================================================================

Dog training should begin with simple commands in a quiet environment with
few distractions. Short and consistent training sessions are generally more
effective than long sessions.

For beginners, training sessions should last approximately 10 to 15 minutes.
Dogs should receive a small reward immediately after successfully completing
a requested behaviour.

The first three commands recommended for a new dog are "sit", "stay", and
"come". Owners should introduce one command at a time before combining
multiple commands in the same training session.

==============================================================================
2. POSITIVE REINFORCEMENT
==============================================================================

Positive reinforcement means rewarding the dog when it performs the desired
behaviour. Rewards may include small treats, verbal praise, toys, or play.

Punishment should not be used as the primary training technique. Consistent
rewards help the dog associate the requested behaviour with a positive
outcome.

The reward should normally be given within two seconds of the correct
behaviour so the dog can clearly associate the reward with the action.

==============================================================================
3. TRAINING FREQUENCY
==============================================================================

Beginner dogs should complete two short training sessions per day. Each
session should focus on one or two behaviours.

Training can gradually become more difficult by introducing distractions,
increasing the duration of commands such as "stay", or practising in new
locations.

Dogs that appear tired, stressed, or uninterested should be given a break
rather than continuing the training session.
